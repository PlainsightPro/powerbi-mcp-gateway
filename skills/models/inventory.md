# Contoso Inventory context (EXAMPLE)

- [Stock Units] and [Stock Value] describe a snapshot. Do not sum snapshots over dates.
- [Days of Cover] estimates how long stock will last under the model's demand calculation.
- [Stock-out Days] counts days without availability in the selected period.
- Use the schema to identify the warehouse, product and snapshot date columns.
- Ask which snapshot or period is intended when it is unclear. Do not apply sales-model definitions here.
